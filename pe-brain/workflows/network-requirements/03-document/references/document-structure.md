# Network requirements document structure

Proposed structure. Replace with the Stone Three template when one is supplied (`[TBC: Danie, s3-agent-knowledge-base]`); keep this file as the single home of the structure.

1. **Purpose and scope.** Site, products and versions, what this document asks of the client.
2. **Summary of requirements.** One table: requirement, owner, needed by.
3. **Zones.** Register Zones table. Where Stone Three equipment sits.
4. **Hosts and IP addresses.** Register Hosts table.
5. **Required flows and firewall rules.** Conduit summary from validation, grouped by zone pair, then the full Flows table.
6. **Time synchronisation.** Source per host, upstream reference, accuracy target.
7. **Remote access.** `[TBC]` unless the register has remote-access flows.
8. **Open items.** Every `[TBC]` with owner.
9. **Diagram.** Mermaid zones and conduits.
10. **Revision table.**
