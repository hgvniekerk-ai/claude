    3 Proposed solution

    3.1 Froth Sensor

    Measurement of individual flotation cell performance is not possible using traditional instrumentation. The Froth Sensor is an industrialised machine-vision system that measures key flotation parameters at each cell where it is installed.

    • Froth velocity over the lip
    • Froth height above the cell lip
    • Froth bubble size
    • Froth stability
    • Froth colour

    3.2 Pulp Sensor

    The Online Pulp Sensor is an in-situ machine-vision system that provides online measurement of key pulp-phase metrics.

    • Gas hold-up (Eg)
    • Bubble size, Sauter mean diameter (D32)
    • Superficial gas velocity (Jg)
    • Bubble surface area flux (Sb)

    6 Commercial terms

    • Validity: 30 days from 12 October 2026 (to 11 November 2026).
    • Payment: 30 days from invoice.
    • Hardware invoicing: 50% on order, 50% when goods are ready for collection (Ex Works).
    • Services invoicing: On completion of each activity.
    • Licence: The annual licence, including remote support, starts when sensor data is available on the OPC server and is invoiced annually in advance from that date.
    • Delivery: Ex Works (EXW), Stone Three, Somerset West. Shipping and insurance to site are excluded.

    7 Assumptions and exclusions

    Items and stages below are outside Stone Three's scope, as set out in section 4.

    • Froth and pulp sensors: Client (install, connect).
    • Field control panels: Client (install).
    • Sensor mounting brackets: Client (supply, install).
    • Power supply to panels: Client (supply, install, connect, commission).
    • Network and fibre: Client (supply, install, connect, commission).
    • Sensor server: Client (supply, install, connect).
    • OPC integration to site systems: Client (connect).
    • Shipping and insurance to site: Client.

    8 Project conditions

    • The client provides a physical or virtual server meeting Stone Three's specification, with remote access available to Stone Three.
    • Stone Three performs site work only after written approval of the visit and confirmation that the site readiness requirements issued by Stone Three have been met.
    • Where site readiness requirements are not met and Stone Three cannot perform contracted tasks while on site, Stone Three may charge standing time at its standard rates.
    • Changes to scope are requested in writing; Stone Three responds with a quotation, and work proceeds only on a new or amended purchase order.
